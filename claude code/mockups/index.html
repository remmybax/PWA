OneTrip screens

Real screenshots of the three apps as they're built today: the driver app on a phone, the admin dashboard and the mechanic portal at desk size. Every screen is the app's own code, filled with fake test data (trucks TEST001 to TEST003). The photos are the real baseline shots of truck 826016.

The driver and admin screens include two changes still waiting to be pushed: photo time and fingerprint recording (driver) and the loading-text, truck-number and error sign-out polish (admin).

For drivers · phone

Driver app

The walk-around itself. Stations unlock in walking order, photos come only from the live camera, and once certified the record locks and uploads to the company Drive.

For Kevin · desktop

Admin dashboard

Read-only review of every inspection in Drive, signed in with a Google account that has view access.

For Emmett · desktop or phone

Mechanic portal

Open defects across all trucks. The mechanic signs off a repair, and the next driver of that truck sees it on screen 3 of the driver app.
