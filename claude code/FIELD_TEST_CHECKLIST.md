# OneTrip field test checklist (Phase 7)

For Dan, on his real phone, on truck 826016. These are the things Claude can't test, because its browser has no camera. Do one real inspection, note what happens at each step, and send the notes back. "Didn't work" is a useful answer; so is "worked, but annoying".

Write down first: phone model, Android or iPhone, and whether OneTrip was opened from the home-screen icon or a browser tab.

## Before starting
- [ ] Open OneTrip. If it was already installed, close and reopen it once so it picks up the newest version.

## Camera (never tested on a real phone since the September restyle)
- [ ] The camera opens at the first photo station (driver door / DOT number).
- [ ] The baseline guide photo shows over the camera at engine-bay and exterior stations, and lines up well enough to frame the shot.
- [ ] **Flash**: Android: does the Flash button turn the phone's light on and off? iPhone: it should say "Flash unavailable". That's expected, because iPhones don't allow it from a web app.
- [ ] **Landscape**: turn the phone sideways while the camera is open. Does the picture rotate to landscape? After closing the camera, does the app go back to portrait? (Android only; iPhones may stay portrait.)
- [ ] Photos look sharp and right-side-up in the app after capture.

## The walk
- [ ] Pass/Fail/N/A taps don't make the page jump or shake.
- [ ] The DOT number typed at the door shows up already filled in at Station 12 in the cab.
- [ ] Anything confusing, slow, or annoying at any station (write the station number).
- [ ] How long the whole inspection took, start to certify.

## Upload
- [ ] After Certify, does the upload finish? Roughly how long?
- [ ] If signal is weak somewhere, note it. Does the upload fail with a Retry button rather than hanging forever?
- [ ] The inspection shows up in the admin dashboard afterward.
