# Monthly Vehicle Inspection: draft checklist

**Draft for Dan to mark up.** Cross out what doesn't apply, add what's missing, and fix the wording to
match how Indeca drivers talk. Nothing here is final, and the legal items should be checked against
Indeca's own safety policy. This is a company monthly inspection, not the DOT annual inspection, but
it checks that the annual and the other dated paperwork are current.

How to read the tables:

- **Record** is what the driver enters: Pass / Fail / N/A, a count, a reading, or a date.
- **Photo**: 📷 = required photo (live camera only, like OneTrip). Blank = no photo needed.
- **Exp** = the app asks for an expiration or due date and flags it on the dashboard when it's
  within 30 days (red when past).
- Any **Fail** needs a note and a photo of the problem, whatever the Photo column says.
